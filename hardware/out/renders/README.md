# K-01 renders

Each image below is a CAD render of the K-01 design made from hardware/cad/pod_k01.py, not a photograph of a built pod. Rendered 2026-11-14 with Blender 4.4.0 (Cycles, CPU) by hardware/render/render_k01.py, meshes from hardware/render/export_meshes.py.

- hero.jpg: CAD render of the design, not a photograph of a built pod. Three-quarter front view with the USB-C cable trailing to the back. 2026-11-14, Blender 4.4.0.
- front.jpg: CAD render of the design, not a photograph of a built pod. Front face with the speaker grille, camera, finger port and K-01. 2026-11-14, Blender 4.4.0.
- top.jpg: CAD render of the design, not a photograph of a built pod. Top with the microphone port and the UV LED. 2026-11-14, Blender 4.4.0.
- rear.jpg: CAD render of the design, not a photograph of a built pod. Rear plate with vents, cable exit, cable clamp and screws. 2026-11-14, Blender 4.4.0.
- underside.jpg: CAD render of the design, not a photograph of a built pod. Pod upside down showing the feet with rubber pads, the lead exit and the air sensor capsule in its clip; the outside run of the USB cable is left out. 2026-11-14, Blender 4.4.0.

Interior views are not in this folder yet. The fit-check previews and an early interior pass with stand-in boards are in hardware/out/engineering_previews/.

Added in the render scene only, not in the CAD: rubber foot pads, countersunk M3 screw heads, the camera lens glass, the cable run on the table and its loose USB-C plug, and surface detail (0.2 mm layer lines, textured bed face, tinned pads on the boards).

Third-party asset: studio_small_09 HDRI (1k) by Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09, licence CC0. Downloaded to hardware/build/hdri/ (not in the repo), used for reflections at low strength.
